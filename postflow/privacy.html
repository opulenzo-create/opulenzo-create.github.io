Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy describes the current local Postflow Windows prototype. Postflow is under development; future hosted versions may use different processing.

1. Operator and contact

Postflow is an independent software project operated under the XZIAEW project name. Privacy questions and requests: xziaew@gmail.com. Postflow is not owned or operated by TikTok, Meta, Google or YouTube.

2. Information processed

When you use the desktop application, it may handle:

  • Your imported videos, filenames, file hashes, resolution, frame rate, duration and validation results.
  • Video descriptions, captions, platform choices, schedules, publication IDs, publishing outcomes and error logs.
  • Authorized connected-account identifiers, OAuth access and refresh tokens, API credentials and necessary information returned by social platform APIs.
  • Information you choose to send in a support email.

Postflow does not require you to type your TikTok, Instagram or Google account password into the desktop app.

3. Purposes and lawful grounds

These data are used for user-requested video management, account connection, scheduled publishing, confirmation of publication, troubleshooting and basic security. Where relevant, processing is based on provision of requested functionality and legitimate interests in secure and reliable operation, subject to applicable law. Social account permissions are obtained through each platform's authorization mechanisms.

4. Local storage and security

The current prototype keeps video copies, metadata, schedules and operational logs locally on the user's computer, including an SQLite database and a video folder. On Windows it encrypts saved API settings and credentials using the Windows Data Protection API for the signed-in Windows user. Keep your computer and backups secure and never expose its local server port to the public internet.

5. Transfers to platforms and website hosting

When you authorize a connection or enable publishing, Postflow sends relevant account and publication data to the official APIs of TikTok, YouTube/Google and Meta/Instagram. Those platforms have their own privacy policies. An Instagram publication may require a publicly accessible video URL hosted separately by the user. Postflow's informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical access information, such as IP addresses and server logs. We do not intentionally add analytics scripts, ads or tracking cookies to these pages.

6. Retention and deletion

Video copies, local schedules and logs remain on the device until they are removed. The current MVP does not include a complete one-click deletion workflow. You can revoke Postflow's access in each social platform's settings, stop the application, and remove local application files and backups you no longer need. Deleting local data does not remove already published social media content. For help, email xziaew@gmail.com.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of personal data. Contact us at the address above. Records saved exclusively on your own device are under your local control.

8. Children

Postflow is for people who may lawfully use the connected social platforms and authorize an app. This informational website is not intended to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as Postflow evolves and will update the date above. Changes to hosting or data practices require a review of this policy.

10. Contact

Postflow / XZIAEW · xziaew@gmail.com
